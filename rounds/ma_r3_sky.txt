2. **Criterion 1 — replace the voluntary refund with a small AVM smart contract escrow.** This is your biggest technical gap and it's ~100 lines of PyTeal/Algorand TypeScript: buyer's payment goes to an app holding the funds; seller app-calls with the review hash to release, or the buyer reclaims after a timeout. 4. **Criterion 5 — enable the CI you already wrote.** A human on the team commits `ci/verify.yml` to `.github/workflows/` (30 seconds in the GitHub web UI; no token needed) and a green check appears proving the on-chain verification test passes continuously.
